Bricks & blocks testing

Strength, water absorption and dimensional tests on bricks, blocks and pavers.

Type
Test
Material
Bricks and blocks
Method
IS 3495

Testing of bricks and blocks shows whether masonry units are suitable for safe, long-lasting construction.

Tests covered

  • Dimensional tolerance
  • Compressive strength
  • Water absorption after 24 hours of immersion
  • Flexural strength
  • Efflorescence
  • Density
  • Initial rate of absorption
  • Concrete block testing
  • Visual check for cracks, voids and chipping
  • Units tested: clay bricks, fly ash bricks, concrete blocks, AAC blocks and interlocking pavers

Typical applications

  • Residential and commercial buildings
  • Masonry construction
  • Pavements and walkways
  • Retaining and partition walls
  • Infrastructure projects
